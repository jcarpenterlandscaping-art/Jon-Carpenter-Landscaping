How It Started

The neighbor asked. Then another. Then another.

It didn't start with a business plan or a logo. It started with a neighbor who needed their lawn mowed and figured Jon was the right person to ask. He did a good job. Word got around — the way it does in a neighborhood when someone actually cares about their work. A few lawns became a few more. A few more became landscaping projects, then stonework, then pergolas. People could tell, somehow, that he wasn't just going through the motions. Thirty-five years later, that's still what sets JC Landscaping apart. Not a slogan. Just the work.

About Jon

He started with a lawn mower and a good reputation.

Jon Carpenter grew up around plants. The kind of kid who'd notice what was wrong with a garden before he noticed anything else. He didn't set out to run a landscaping company — that happened gradually, the way most real things do. A neighbor asked him to mow. He did a good job. Another neighbor heard about it. Then another.

But lawn mowing was never really the point. Landscaping was. Seeing a yard and knowing what it needs — what shape it wants to take, what plants belong there, how light moves through it at different times of day. That's the part that gets him up in the morning.

"I'm not trying to impress anybody. I just want the work to speak for itself."

— Jon Carpenter

35 Years of Paying Attention

Thirty-five years in, Jon still walks every property before he picks up a tool. He wants to understand it first. What's working. What's struggling. What the owners actually want to do out there. He's not in a rush to start — because starting wrong is a lot harder to fix than taking a few extra minutes at the beginning.

His crew shares that same mindset. They don't just do the work — they care about how it looks when they're done. Every edge, every stone, every planted bed. If something's not right, they say so and they fix it. That's not a company policy. It's just how Jon raised them to work.
