Service

Hardscaping

Built solid, built right — the structural backbone of a great yard.

Good hardscaping is what holds a yard together. The walls, the borders, the structure — when it's done right, you don't really notice it. It just feels solid and intentional. When it's done wrong, you notice it immediately: uneven edges, walls that lean, borders that wash out after the first hard rain. We don't cut corners on the foundation work.

From tiered retaining walls to decorative garden borders, we approach every hardscaping project the same way — by understanding the ground first. Proper grading, drainage planning, and base preparation are what make the difference between a wall that lasts 30 years and one that needs to be rebuilt in five.

Whether you're dealing with a slope that needs taming, a garden that needs definition, or a yard that needs structural bones before the planting can begin, our crew can handle it. The stonework is tight, the lines are clean, and the finished product looks like it belongs.

"The wall has to be right before anything else goes in. Get that wrong and you're redoing everything."

Stonework

Meticulous, artistic, and permanent — stone placed the way it wants to sit.

Stonework is the part of this trade that demands the most care. There's a craft to it that goes beyond function — the way each piece sits, the way the pattern flows, the way the finished pathway or wall looks like it could have been there for a hundred years. That's not an accident. It comes from knowing stone.

We hand-select material for every stonework project. We know the difference between a stone that wants to sit flat and one that needs to be positioned on edge. We understand how stone weathers, how it handles freeze-thaw cycles, and which materials hold their character over time. That knowledge shows in the work.

Pathways, steps, garden borders, decorative placement — our stonework ranges from simple to elaborate, but they share the same standard. Every joint is tight. Every surface is stable. Every line was thought about. If you want stonework that still looks right twenty years from now, this is the crew to call.

"Stone has a right way to sit. Once you learn to see it, you can't unsee it — and you can't settle for less."

Pergolas

Custom-built outdoor structures — a place to breathe, gather, and settle in.

A pergola is one of those additions that changes how you use your yard. It gives the space definition — suddenly there's a reason to be outside. A place to sit in the shade, have dinner, or just breathe without the sun in your face. We build pergolas that feel like they belong to the property, not like something dropped in from a big-box store.

Every pergola we build is custom — sized and positioned for how you actually want to use it. We handle all the framing, post-setting, and finishing work using durable hardwoods suited to the build. The result is solid enough to outlast most of what's around it, and built well enough that you won't be looking at it wondering if it's level.

Whether you're looking for a simple four-post structure over a patio, a more elaborate design with built-in seating, or something that connects to the house — we'll figure out what makes sense for your space. Come spring, when everyone else is wishing they'd done it last year, you'll be glad you did it now.

"A pergola should feel like it grew there. That takes planning, and it takes building it right the first time."

Fencing

Clean lines, solid build — done right the first time, every time.

Fencing is one of those things that's easy to do badly. Crooked posts, gaps at the bottom, rails that sag — these things stand out every single day. We've been building fences for 35 years and we've never gotten comfortable with sloppy work. Posts go in plumb, rails go in level, and the finished line is something you can actually be proud of looking at from either side.

Whether it's a privacy fence to block out a neighbor, a decorative border to define your property, or a functional enclosure for a dog run or garden, we build it the right way. Proper post depth, proper concrete, and proper spacing — the basics that most people cut corners on and regret later. Material options depend on your budget and goals — we'll walk you through what makes sense.

We also handle fence repairs and replacements. If you've got sections that are leaning, rotting out, or just looking rough, our crew can assess what needs to come out and what can be salvaged. A straight fence line makes the whole yard look better — it's one of those upgrades that's obvious the day it's done.

"A good fence line makes the whole yard look intentional. There's no shortcut to getting it straight."

Patios

Rock and stone patios built to last — and built to be the centerpiece of your yard.

We build rock and stone patios that become the centerpiece of the backyard. Not the kind that shifts and sinks after a winter — the kind that's still perfectly level a decade later because the base was done right. Every patio project starts below the surface: proper excavation, compacted gravel, and a screeded sand bed that gives the stone a foundation it can actually sit on.

Material selection matters as much as installation. We work with fieldstone, bluestone, flagstone, and natural irregular rock depending on the look you're after and the budget you're working with. We'll walk you through the options and help you understand what each material looks like over time — because some stones weather beautifully and some just get stained. You should know what you're getting before it goes in the ground.

The finished patio is tight, flat, and drains correctly. We don't rush the setting process — each stone gets positioned, checked, adjusted, and checked again. If something's not right, it comes back up. That standard is what makes the difference between a patio that looks great at installation and one that still looks great five years later.

"The base is everything. If the foundation isn't right, nothing else matters. That's where we spend our time."
